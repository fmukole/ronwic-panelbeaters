Insurance claims

Claiming from insurance? We help you through it.

An accident is stressful enough without the paperwork. Bring us your insurance details and we help you fill in the claim forms, then deal with your insurer by email or phone while the car is with us.

  • Free quote for your insurer. We prepare it at our workshop, or we can come to you.
  • Help with the forms. All you need is your insurance details.
  • Updates while we work. We keep you posted from approval to handover.
  • Staying mobile. Courtesy cars when available, plus a free drop-off and collection service.
  • Help with the excess. Ask us about paying it over three months. Terms and conditions apply.

Not claiming? We do private repairs too, with our best prices on cash jobs.

Our story

Half a century of straightening Cape Town’s cars

Ronwic Motors has been panel beating and spray painting in Cape Town since 1974. From our workshop at 22 Basil Crescent in Ottery East, we repair accident damage for insurers and private customers, on everything from small hatchbacks to working bakkies.

Customers tell us that what they value most is being kept in the loop: a straight answer on the quote, help with the insurance paperwork, and regular updates until the car is ready. Some of them have been coming back for more than 15 years.

TimOwner, Ronwic Motors CC
